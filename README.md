# RafRadar

A digital shelf audit for brands: how visible is a brand in e-commerce search results (Trendyol, N11)? It measures findability, rank, share of shelf and price consistency, and turns them into a dashboard, a report and an action list.

## Problem
Brands measure eye level on the physical shelf, but not the online shelf a shopper actually sees: the first 20 search results. A typical finding: people who search by brand name find the product, people who search by category do not.

## Result
- Sample run: 24 searches, 1,222 listings, average organic share of shelf 4.1%.
- Used in a trade marketing interview case.
